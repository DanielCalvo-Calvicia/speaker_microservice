## Goal

Prove on the real output device of the Windows PC what only the fake driver has shown since the last passes (R-10): `tests/simple.py`, the device-rate resampling, supersession, `stop_playback`, and the readiness endpoints. The R-6 drain fix was already confirmed on 2026-11-09 and is not repeated here.

## Read first

`tests/simple.py`, [ADR-006](../../decisions/ADR-006-audio-never-converted-resample-on-device-rejection.md), [ADR-008](../../decisions/ADR-008-new-playback-supersedes-the-running-one.md), the real-device run described in R-6.

## Steps (say before each sound: the volume)

1. Tell the maintainer, before anything: the steps play short sounds on the default output device; ask for a low volume and a "go". Check ports 7998 to 8005 are free.
2. Run from a **scratch copy** of the working tree (not the working folder): `tests\simple.py` with the service venv, and note each check.
3. Resampling: play a clip at a rate the device rejects (the real device accepted 24 kHz mono on 2026-11-09; try 22,050 and 8,000 Hz generated tones, short) and listen: normal pitch and speed, no truncation; read the log line `Output device rate differs from the stream rate; resampling` if it appears.
4. Supersession: start a long tone and send a second request; the first stops at once and the second plays (ADR-008).
5. `GET /ready` and `GET /available` before, during and after playback; `POST`/stop path if the route exists.
6. Stop the service and check the device is released (another program can play).

## Acceptance criteria

Each step is recorded with the date, the device (name, no serial) and what was heard; failures become tasks, not silent fixes. Delete the scratch folder and any generated audio at the end; no service left running; ports free.

## Result (2026-11-10, run with the maintainer at the PC, volume low, who said "go")

**Machine:** this Windows PC, default output device index 3 (the selector found no keyword match and fell back to the OS default, as in F13); from a scratch copy of the working tree with a made-up `.env` (port 8103, `SPEAKER_BACKEND=real`), never the real one; the audio was generated tones. **Step `tests/simple.py`:** exit 0, health ok, `stream_started` then `completed` (24 kHz mono, 2 s of 440 Hz), **96,000 of 96,000 bytes written**; the maintainer heard the beep. **Sample rates:** 1.5 s of 880 Hz sent at 22,050 Hz and at 8,000 Hz were both accepted by the device at those rates (`Speaker started` with that format, no `Output device rate differs` line, so the resampler was **not** exercised: this device took both rates) and written in full (66,150 and 24,000 bytes); the maintainer heard normal pitch and speed, no clicks or gaps. **Probes:** `/ready` and `/available` answered ready and available (reason `null`) before, during and after playback. **Supersession:** a 6 s tone replaced after 1.5 s by a 1 s tone: the maintainer heard the low tone cut off by the higher one, then silence. **Release:** the service was terminated after each run and the next run opened the device again at once. **A defect was found:** a playback that was **replaced while it was still draining** (its whole body already queued, the device part-way through it, then a newer request) did not end at once: its drain wait could never finish because the newer request had cancelled its worker, so it held the caller's request for the whole bound (8.24 s for 6 s of audio) and, with the T-01.6 change, answered `success: false` (`Playback did not finish: the device played 0 of 288000 bytes ...`), which Brain would have taken for a failed playback; before T-01.6 it answered success after the same delay. A second flaw sat next to it: when that wait ended, the old session reset `_is_playing` and `_current_session`, which belong to the newer one, so it could have cut the newer playback short. `_wait_for_drain` now ends with `replaced` as soon as the session is no longer the current one (looked at every 0.1 s), the session answers "Playback session superseded by new request" (success) and leaves the shared state alone. Tests written first (the first failed on the old code with `success: False`): `test_a_playback_replaced_while_it_drains_ends_at_once_as_superseded_not_as_a_failure` and `test_the_end_of_a_replaced_playback_does_not_cut_the_newer_one_short`; 8 of 8 one-line mutations of the new code caught; 120 tests, ruff 7 findings and mypy 4 errors as before. **Re-run on the device after the fix (the supersession step only):** the first request ended after **1.69 s** with "Playback session superseded by new request", the log has `Playback session superseded while draining` and no `truncating` warning, the second tone (1 s) was written in full (48,000 of 48,000 bytes) and the maintainer confirmed what he heard: a low tone cut off after about 1.5 s by a higher tone for about 1 s, nothing playing afterwards. **Not exercised:** the device-rate resampler (the device accepted every rate; it needs a device that rejects one), `/process/stream/set` driven by Brain, Linux. The scratch copy and its logs (no speech) were deleted, nothing was left running, ports 7998 to 8005 and 8103 are free.
